Juzu / 数珠

Japanese Buddhist prayer beads · 108 beads · one for every human desire

A juzu is not simply jewelry — it is a meditation object, a talisman, a daily practice worn on the wrist or carried in the hand. RACA's juzu are handmade, each strand singular in its bead selection, stone, and intention. They are the most personal thing Cara makes.

Stones are chosen for their energy and beauty: labradorite for transformation, tiger's eye for clarity, malachite for protection, obsidian for grounding. Each juzu is unique — the stones, the tassel, the knot, the intention behind it — and every commission begins with a conversation.

About RACA

Made by hand.
Made with intention.

RACA is the jewelry practice of Cara, a California-based metalsmith and designer. Every piece — from the most delicate layering chain to the boldest sculptural collar — is made by hand, one at a time.

RACA exists in two registers: Essentials, for the pieces you wear every day without thinking, and Studio, for the pieces you wear when you want to say something. Both are made with the same care, the same materials, the same hands.

Featured in French Vogue during Paris Fashion Week and shown at the American Craft Council, RACA's studio work has been worn by collectors and artists across the US.
